01 / THE CHALLENGE

People need practical ways to stay connected when travelling.

01

Disconnected contacts

Important contact information may be scattered across different devices and conversations.

02

Limited journey visibility

Friends and family may not know when a journey is expected to end.

03

Unclear next steps

People may not have quick access to relevant local safety resources.

02 / THE PLATFORM

Safety tools built around trusted connections.

SafeSpace is designed to support personal safety planning. It does not replace emergency services, professional security or local authorities.

WHY NOW

Digital safety tools are becoming part of everyday life.

Connected devices create opportunities for better personal safety coordination. SafeSpace must prioritise consent, location privacy, security, reliability and clear emergency limitations.
